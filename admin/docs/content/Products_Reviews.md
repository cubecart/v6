# Product Reviews

## Review List

All product reviews with their title, product, star rating, reviewer details, and approval status. Sort by rating or date. Use the search tab to filter by keyword, product, or approval status.

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Title | The review headline. Click to edit. |
| Product | The product being reviewed. Click to go to the product. |
| Rating | Star rating from 1 to 5. |
| Name | Reviewer name. Shows "Anonymous" if the review was submitted anonymously. |
| Approved | Toggle to approve or reject the review. |

## Add Review

Manually create a review by searching for a product, entering reviewer details, writing the review text, and selecting a star rating.

## Bulk Delete

Remove all reviews matching a specific email address or IP address. Useful for clearing spam reviews.

## Search

Search reviews by keyword, filter by product, or filter by approval status (all, approved, or unapproved).
