# Product Statistics

Detailed sales data for an individual product.

## Overview

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Date Added | When the product was created. |
| Last Updated | When the product was last modified. |
| First Sale | Date of the earliest order containing this product. |
| Last Sale | Date of the most recent order containing this product. |
| Total Sales | Total units sold. Click to view the matching orders. If more than one sale, the average interval between sales is shown. |

## Date Range Filter

Use the From and To date pickers to narrow the statistics to a specific period.

## Customers Who Purchased

A paginated list of customers who have bought this product, showing their name, email, and total number of purchases. Click a customer to view their account.
